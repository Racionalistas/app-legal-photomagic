Photo Magic AI Enhancer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Effective: August 30, 2026

Photo Magic AI Enhancer has no accounts, no sign-in, and no user database. We never ask for your email.

Product analytics and diagnostics (PostHog) may be collected to understand feature usage and improve reliability. We do not use advertising tracking or session replay and do not sell personal information.

Your history and settings stay on your device. Uninstalling Photo Magic AI Enhancer removes local data; email us if you want anonymous analytics records deleted.

1. Who We Are

Photo Magic AI Enhancer is developed and operated by Photo Magic Inc. (referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). For data protection purposes, we are the data controller for the limited information described below.

Contact: support@photomagic.app

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where that information lives, and what rights you have. It applies to the Photo Magic AI Enhancer mobile application only.

2. Information We Collect

No accounts: Photo Magic AI Enhancer does not offer account creation and does not support sign-in of any kind. We do not collect your email address, phone number, postal address, or payment details.

Subscription information: RevenueCat may receive an anonymous customer identifier, product, entitlement, purchase and expiration dates, renewal state, and transaction environment. Apple processes payment details; Photo Magic AI Enhancer does not receive full card numbers.

Product analytics: PostHog may receive lifecycle events, feature interactions, paywall and purchase-flow events, error details, and device/app context. We disable session replay and do not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Local device information: onboarding completion, analytics-consent choice, and interface preferences stored on your device.

3. What The App Does Not Do

  • No advertising and no cross-app tracking. There are no ad networks, no IDFA/advertising identifier access, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because the App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months.
  • No user database. We operate no server that stores records about individual users.
  • No push notifications. The App does not send push notifications and does not request notification permission.

5. Legal Bases and Analytics Consent

Product analytics and diagnostics are used to understand feature usage, improve reliability, and fix problems. The App does not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can contact us at the address below with privacy questions or requests.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases for processing are: performance of a contract with you, for delivering the App's functionality and your subscription; and our legitimate interests, for anonymous analytics used to keep the App working, fix problems, and improve it. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time using the contact address below.

6. Retention and Deletion

On-device data is kept for as long as the App is installed. Deleting Photo Magic AI Enhancer from your device permanently deletes all of it — your settings, history, and data. This is immediate and requires no request to us. Because there is no account, Apple's in-app account deletion requirement does not apply. If you nevertheless want the anonymous analytics record for your installation erased, email us at support@photomagic.app and we will delete it. Anonymous analytics events are retained on a rolling basis for as long as they remain useful for product analysis, and no longer than 24 months. Subscription records are retained by RevenueCat and Apple for as long as required for billing, tax, and accounting purposes. Limited records may remain temporarily in encrypted backups or where required for security, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, disputes, or other legal obligations. They are removed or anonymized when the applicable retention period ends.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you, and receive a copy of it
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete information
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact support@photomagic.app. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The App's design is its main security property: sensitive material stays on your device and is never transmitted. Data in transit to the service providers listed above is encrypted using HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version. If we make a material change to how information is handled, we will make that clear in the App before the change takes effect.
